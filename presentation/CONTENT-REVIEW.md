## Specific content decisions / unresolved questions

1. **The Promenide:** retained exactly as supplied. Confirm spelling before public launch.
2. **Veranda Sessions / District Sessions:** source slide 18 uses Veranda Sessions; source slide 21 uses District Sessions. The deck consistently uses Veranda Sessions, matching the programme brief and user request. Final programme nomenclature needs confirmation.
3. **Masterbrand:** Batam Commons is the source’s creative recommendation, not an approved final name. Canopy Quarter, Garden Quarter and The Courtyard remain candidates. Naming clearance and linguistic checks have not been performed.
4. **Spatial image:** `SLIDE 11/sapital.png` identifies itself as a conceptual spatial overlay, not a measured architectural plan. The new relationship diagram is explicitly conceptual and not to scale, preserving the central gathering place, connections and quieter layer. An approved site plan was not available.
5. **Supplied imagery:** architectural/lifestyle visuals are treated as concept imagery. Their creators, approvals and as-built status have not been verified. `SLIDE 2` contains context photographs; the Nagoya Hill image appears once on the “Discover Further” journey and is explicitly identified as a city reference, not a KALINDO facility.
6. **Batam Table:** the supplied culinary image illustrates gathering and food discovery; it is not evidence of an installed shared-table facility or confirmed event. Proposed formats are listed separately.
7. **Family activities:** managed children’s supervision is distinguished from shared family experiences. It is not assumed to be an operating service.
8. **Programming:** frequency, capacity, costs, scheduling and operational feasibility remain for operator review. No calendar dates are implied.
