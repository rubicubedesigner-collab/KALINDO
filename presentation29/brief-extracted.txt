SLIDE 03 — POLLUX MALL BATAM CENTER

FUNCTIONAL VISITS SUPPORT EVERYDAY RELEVANCE

Public evidence
The official store list includes MAKO, J.CO, fashion brands, optical retailers and electronics.
Immigration Batam also confirms passport and stay-permit services at the Pollux
Immigration Lounge.

Source : https://polluxmalls.com/polluxhabibiemall-store-list/?utm_source=

Commercial interpretation

   ● Services create a specific reason to visit.
   ● Everyday F&B and retail can capture spending around that visit.
   ● The mixed-use setting offers a potential local customer base.

Implication for Kalindo Project
Useful services should contribute to the district’s weekday relevance.

Further validation
Service-user conversion into tenant spending, weekday footfall and current vacancy.

Visual: Diagram sederhana hubungan service visit dengan café/retail. Jangan
mengasumsikan captive residents otomatis menjadi pelanggan aktif.




SLIDE 04 — NAGOYA HILL

A BROAD MIX SUPPORTS MULTIPLE VISIT MISSIONS

Public evidence
Nagoya Hill’s official website displays Hypermart, Matahari, cinema, F&B, electronics, optical
and service brands. Its official description places the superblock within a surrounding
concentration of hotels and commercial uses.

Source : https://nagoyahill.co.id/?utm_source=

Commercial interpretation

   ●   Grocery supports routine visits.
   ●   Shopping and entertainment create additional occasions.
   ●   Dining and services allow several needs within one trip.
   ●   Surrounding hospitality broadens the potential catchment.

Implication for Kalindo Project
Build several dependable reasons to return, supported by distinctive experiences.

Further validation
Comparable occupancy, tenant turnover, visitor origin and daypart performance.

Visual: Visit missions: groceries, dining, services, entertainment.

PROGRAMMING ADDS REASONS TO VISIT

Public evidence

involving 40 businesses with culinary and entertainment activities. It also records a February

https://disbudpar.batam.go.id/2026/04/28/bazar-kuliner-dan-hiburan-wonderfood-ram

SLIDE 26 — REVENUE SCENARIOS

THREE SCENARIOS, BUILT FROM THE SAME INVENTORY

  Scenario                                    Working basis

 Conservativ     Slower occupancy, lower achieved rents and lower activation utilisation
 e

 Base            Current assumptions, adjusted as evidence becomes available

 Upside          Supported improvements in occupancy, rent and commercial utilisation

Bottom-up additional revenue

   ●   Events: available booking slots × booked share × fee.
   ●   Media: assets × rate × sell-through.
   ●   Activations: locations × booked days × rate.
   ●   Sponsorship: contracted packages and recognised period.
   ●   Tourism: fulfilled bookings × net fee or commission.

Catatan: Pendapatan event, sponsorship dan activation harus dicek agar tidak menghitung
